Accessibility

We aim to make Airlines usable for as many people as possible. The site uses semantic structure, keyboard-focus styles, and respects reduced-motion preferences where animations are used.

If you hit a barrier (e.g. a form, contrast, or screen-reader issue), tell us via the contact section and we will work to fix it. Partner booking sites have their own accessibility practices when you leave our pages.

Transparency

Affiliate & advertising disclosure

Airlines may earn a commission when you book flights, transfers, or activities through links on this site (including Aviasales, GetTransfer, and Klook via Travelpayouts). That does not increase the price you are offered on the partner site.

We do not sell airline tickets ourselves. Sample flight results and prices on this page are for demonstration of the interface only. The partner’s site shows the binding price, taxes, and terms before you pay.

Advertising or sponsored placements, if added later, will be labeled. This disclosure is part of how we keep the product honest—not a substitute for accurate pricing or lawful data use.
